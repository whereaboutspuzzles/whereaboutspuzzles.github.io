2. In short

This website sets no cookies, loads no analytics or advertising scripts, and loads nothing from third parties (no embedded fonts, videos or maps). 3. Hosting on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare; counting page views

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. It is delivered through the servers of Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which, as our processor (Art. 28 GDPR), receive every request and pass it on to GitHub. When you open a page, Cloudflare processes your IP address, the address requested, your browser's technically necessary details (such as the browser type and language) and the date and time, to deliver the page to you and to protect the website from attacks. Cloudflare does not pass your IP address on to GitHub; GitHub logs the requests for security purposes but sees only Cloudflare. If you open the address whereaboutspuzzles.github.io (for example through the QR code in our books), your request first reaches GitHub directly, which forwards you to this website and logs your IP address in doing so. We do not receive any of these logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More is in GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy. Data is transferred to the USA in the process; GitHub and Cloudflare are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR).

To know how many people open which pages and where they come from, we count page views on Cloudflare's servers. For each page view, a small program there stores only: the page opened, the domain of the page you came from (not its full address), your country, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer), whether the request came from a browser or an automated program, a source tag from the link (utm_source) if it carries one, the response status and the time. Your IP address is neither stored nor analysed for this. No cookies are set, no scripts are loaded, nothing is stored on or read from your device, and page views cannot be linked to one person. The count is deleted after three months. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how our website is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The page /review/ and similar addresses printed in our books forward you straight to Amazon. From there, Amazon's privacy policy applies.
