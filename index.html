Ashcombe Manor, 1931

Every Friday for a year, Lord Ashcombe's guests played a game. The lamps went out for one minute. When they came back on, one guest lay dead, and nobody had moved.

From what each guest said, work out where everyone stood, and who was alone with the body. Fifty nights at Ashcombe Manor, one murderer each time.

How to play

One rule, three steps

  1. Read what each guest said. Every statement is true.

  2. Place one guest in every row and every column. No numbers, only people.

  3. The guest alone with the body is the murderer. Name them.
