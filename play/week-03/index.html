How to play

One murder, one rule, one answer. No guessing needed.

Step 1

One suspect in every row and every column. No two share a row or a column.

Step 2

Every statement is true. "Beside" means the next square up, down, left or right, in the same area.

Step 3

The one alone with the body did it. Exactly one suspect was in the area with the body.

On this page

  • Tap or click a portrait under the plan to pick a suspect, then tap a square: they could be there.
  • Press and hold a square, or switch to Place and tap: they were there.
  • Nobody marks a square no one can be on. Placed suspects stripe their row and column.
  • Check off a statement once you have used it. Stuck? Hints open one at a time.
