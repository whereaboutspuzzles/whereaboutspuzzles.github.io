Miss Pell's case of the week · No. 1

The answer: The Empty Chair

A wet October evening, with rain on every window. When the lamps came back on, Harold lay in the Dining Room, and nobody had moved an inch.

Step by step

Every deduction, in order

  1. 1. Clue 1: Olive has 20 squares left.

  2. 2. Clue 2: Agatha has 11 squares left.

  3. 3. Clue 3: Edith must be somewhere in column 5.

  4. 4. Clue 4: Sam has 19 squares left.

  5. 5. Clue 5: Nora has 19 squares left.

  6. 6. Clue 6: Lucy must be somewhere in row 1.

  7. 7. Clue 7 settles it: Lucy is at R1C3.

  8. 8. Olive has only one square left: R2C2.

  9. 9. Edith has only one square left: R6C5.

  10. 10. Agatha has only one square left: R3C1.

  11. 11. Sam is the only suspect who can still stand in column 6, so they must be somewhere in it.

  12. 12. Sam has only one square left: R5C6.

  13. 13. Only one square is left for Nora, so Nora is at R4C4.

Who did it?

With the grid full, only Nora was in the Dining Room with Harold. Nora is the murderer.
